Poker Guide

Master Every Poker Game

Learn the best strategies, starting hands, and winning habits for every poker variant you play.

Texas Hold’em — Best Practices

  • Play tight from early position. Fold weak hands and avoid trouble hands like K‑J, Q‑J, A‑9 offsuit.
  • Raise your strong hands preflop. Don’t limp with A‑K, A‑Q, or big pairs.
  • Continuation bet only when the board favors you. Dry boards = good. Wet boards = dangerous.
  • Don’t slowplay big hands on draw-heavy boards. Charge draws.
  • Position is everything. Play more hands on the button.

Omaha — Best Practices

  • Play hands that work together. Double‑suited, connected, coordinated cards.
  • Avoid trashy pairs. Aces without support are dangerous.
  • Draws are stronger than made hands. Don’t fall in love with one pair.
  • Nut draws only. Second‑best draws lose money.

Courchevel — Best Practices

  • The exposed flop card changes everything. Adjust immediately.
  • Strong starting hands matter more because one flop card is known.
  • Nut draws dominate — same as Omaha but amplified.

Badeucy — Best Practices

  • Start with strong 2‑way hands (A‑2‑3‑4 + strong Badugi potential).
  • Don’t chase weak Badugis — you’ll get crushed.
  • Draw aggressively when you have position.

5‑Card Draw — Best Practices

  • Only play premium starting hands — big pairs, trips, or strong draws.
  • Don’t draw to inside straights.
  • Bet your strong pairs — don’t give free cards.

Mixed Games — Best Practices

  • Tighten up on games you’re weak at.
  • Attack players who don’t understand the rotation.
  • Track which games you win the most in.
